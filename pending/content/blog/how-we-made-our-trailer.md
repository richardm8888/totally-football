Board games are hard to show on camera.

Nothing moves on its own. The drama of a match lives in players' heads: the bluff before a duel, the groan when a chaos card lands, the celebration when a last-minute shot goes in.

So when we sat down to make a trailer for [Totally Football, our tactical football board game](/), the challenge was clear: how do you make cards on a table feel like a football match?

Here's how we did it.
