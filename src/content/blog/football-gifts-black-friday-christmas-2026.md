*Board games, stocking fillers and present ideas for every kind of football fan.*

Buying for a football fan sounds easy until you realise they already own the shirt, the scarf and the mug.

The best football gifts tap into *how* someone loves the game. The tactician who pauses the match to explain a pressing trap wants something different from the fan who never misses a home game, or the friend whose idea of a perfect evening is a competitive game night.

With Black Friday on 27 November and Christmas not far behind, here are our football gift ideas for 2026, grouped by the kind of fan you're buying for.


## Football board games: the gift that gets everyone playing

A football board game turns a solo hobby into something the whole table can share. It's also a gift that keeps paying off long after Christmas Day, every time it comes out for a game night or a rainy Boxing Day.

Board games are often included in Black Friday sales, so it's a good week to pick one up.

### For the classic fan: Subbuteo

The original flick-to-kick football game. It rewards practice and skill, and for many fans it's pure nostalgia. Best for someone with a bit of table space and the patience to master it.

### For the armchair manager: Superclub

If they've lost whole weekends to football management video games, Superclub brings that feeling to the table: transfers, youth prospects and building a club over several seasons.

### For quicker game nights: Superclub Rivals

A faster, more competitive take on club management. It's a better fit for shorter sessions and players who want direct rivalry from the first turn.

### For the strategist: Sunday Manager

A slower, management-focused game about running a club across a season. Ideal for fans who love the planning side of football more than the ninety minutes.

For a deeper look at each of these, read our guide to the [best football board games in 2026](/blog/best-football-board-games-2026/).

## Football gifts by type of fan

Not every football fan wants a game. Here are ideas that suit different kinds of supporter.

### The tactician

They talk about half-spaces and inverted full-backs at the dinner table.

- A well-reviewed book on football tactics or the history of the game
- A tactics board or whiteboard for their five-a-side team
- A strategy board game that rewards outthinking an opponent

### The matchday regular

Home and away, whatever the weather.

- Tickets to a match they've been wanting to see
- A stadium tour of a ground on their bucket list
- Practical matchday kit: a good flask, a seat cushion or a warm hat in club colours

### The retro fan

They miss the old kits, the old grounds and the old Subbuteo set.

- A retro shirt from a season they still talk about
- A classic flicking game or vintage-style football print
- A book about a legendary team or era

### The five-a-side player

- A new ball or training equipment
- Shin pads, socks or a kit bag
- A post-match game night: a football board game to settle who really was man of the match


## Football stocking fillers

Smaller gifts for under the tree:

- Football card games and quiz packs
- Club-themed socks, keyrings or mugs
- A football annual or quiz book
- A matchday programme from a memorable game


## Black Friday tips for football gifts

- **Make a list before the sales start.** Knowing exactly what you want makes it easier to spot a genuine deal.
- **Check the normal price.** A "deal" isn't always a discount, so compare against what the item usually sells for.
- **Look at independent game shops.** Many run their own Black Friday offers and give great advice on which board games suit which players.
- **Order early for Christmas.** Popular games can sell out in December, and posting gets slower the closer you get to the big day.

The best football gift is one that matches how someone loves the game. For many fans that's a shirt or a ticket. For the ones who love the rivalry, the tactics and the drama, it's a game they can play with the people around them.
